How to join

Joining the club is simple and only takes a few steps. They are:

  1. Apply and create a Volunteer Connection account

    Volunteer Connection is the official system the Red Cross uses to manage volunteers and log service hours. Start your application: it only takes a few minutes.

  2. Have a parent or guardian give consent

    After you apply, the Red Cross emails your parent or guardian a consent form. They'll need to fill in their contact information and accept the terms before your registration is complete.

  3. Watch for your activation email

    Once your application and consent are processed, you'll get an email with a link to activate your account and see any remaining steps.

  4. Attend a meeting

    Come to Room 7206 on the first Monday of the month, 11:15 AM, and learn about upcoming events.

  5. Join the club communication networks

    Follow us on Instagram and join Remind to receive regular updates on events and meeting updates.
